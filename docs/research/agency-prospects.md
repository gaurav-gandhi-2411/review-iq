# Agency prospects (M2a)

Date of research: 2026-10-07. Method: WebSearch + WebFetch of each agency's public page, zero-cost,
no outreach, no logins. Branch: docs/s19-agency-prospects.

## How to read this

- "Seen" = a fetch of the agency's own page returned the stated text on 2026-10-07. The fetch tool
  returns a model-written summary of the page, not raw HTML, so figures and quotes should be
  re-checked on the live page before any external use.
- "Search-only" = appeared in a search result snippet; the agency's own page was NOT fetched.
- "Not stated" = not visible on the page(s) fetched. It is not evidence of absence.
- Brand counts are the agencies' own marketing claims, unaudited. Different pages of the same
  agency disagree (e.g. Brand Central: 300+ in a search snippet, 200+ on the fetched homepage).
- The "review mgmt" column is limited to what the page text said about reviews/ratings. Most
  agencies are listing/ads/ops shops; review handling, where present, is solicitation and rating
  monitoring, not analysis of review text.
- Sweep scope: searches for Amazon/Flipkart/Myntra/Nykaa/quick-commerce account-management
  agencies, listicles (SW Cybernetics guide, Data4Amazon, Trigital) and directories. This is a
  convenience sample, not a census. Clutch/GoodFirms/Semrush directory pages were mostly not
  fetchable (403 or redirect).

## Observations (what the data does and does not support)

- Of 25, only 3 mention review handling on pages actually seen (SW Cybernetics, Global Websters,
  Digital Dawn), plus Eshopbox in a blog. None mention analysing review text, language handling, or
  Hinglish. "Review management" in this market reads as soliciting reviews and watching star
  ratings.
- Brand counts are marketing claims and inconsistent in places. Largest claimed: SW Cybernetics,
  Global Websters, EcomSarthi at about 500. Realistic multi-brand D2C targets with named clients and
  modest scale: Seventh Triangle (40+), GrowthxX (25+), Brains & Brands (15+), Mosaic Brands,
  Calibray.
- Digital Dawn's product IS review/rating management; evaluate as partner or competitor rather than
  customer.
- Contact routes are all public (email/phone/forms). No contact was made.
- UNVERIFIED across the board: whether any of these agencies feel the pain Samidha addresses. See
  demand-evidence.md; nothing here shows they do.
