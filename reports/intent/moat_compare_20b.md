## Setup

- Labels: four-judge panel consensus (SILVER), not human gold. Sample: the first 300 sealed ids in sealed order, MATCHED per task across all compared arms; a sample this small gives wide intervals and coverage at a precision floor is a knife-edge statistic.
- Encoder: the run-2 model (`reports/intent/run2`, the checkpoint behind `model_report.md`; run 3 was a registered negative result and is not promoted). Score = maximum temperature-scaled class probability. Decision mode: the SAME confidence-ordered Wilson-lower-bound rule as for the LLM arms, evaluated in-sample on the matched ids (an operating point, not a deployable threshold). The deployable reading (threshold chosen on validation, stored in `eval_run2.json`) is given separately below.
- LLM arms: verbalised integer confidence. Empty or unparseable answers count wrong at confidence 0 and stay in the denominator. Few-shot confidences are partly copied from the shots (confound, quantified below). The two local arms ran different Ollama builds.
- Rule: operating point = highest-coverage threshold with Wilson 95 lower bound of precision at least the target, whole tie groups; paired item bootstrap with one index set per replicate for all arms. A verdict `beats` needs the bootstrap interval to exclude 0 AND the operating points to agree in direction; otherwise `not separated`.

- It shows (VERIFIED, above): under the pre-registered rule and on these matched silver-labelled items, which pairs separate on coverage at the Wilson-lower-bound operating point and on accuracy, with the intervals.
- It does not show human-gold quality: labels are a four-judge panel consensus. It does not show 120b behaviour or any other model.
- The sample is 300 sealed items for three arms (few-shot stopped there by the registered rule). Intervals are wide; a `not separated` verdict is absence of evidence of a difference, not evidence of equivalence (BELIEVED to be the usual reading for small n).
- The operating point is chosen in-sample on the evaluated items for every arm, which flatters all arms; it is a symmetric comparison, not a deployable guarantee. The deployable encoder reading is the validation-threshold table.
- LLM confidence is verbalised and coarse; with coarse ties the whole-tie-group rule makes coverage jump.
