Managing reviews for one brand is manageable. For twelve, it isn't.

🌐

Half your reviews aren't in English

Real Indian marketplace reviews mix English and romanized Hindi in the same sentence — Hinglish. Off-the-shelf sentiment tools built for English miss the actual complaint buried in the Hinglish half.

🚨

The urgent one is buried in the pile

A safety complaint or a "caught fire" review looks identical to a routine three-star gripe until someone reads it — by which point a day has usually already passed.

🎭

Some of it isn't even real

Coordinated fake-review campaigns are a live problem for Indian D2C brands. Spotting one manually across a dozen storefronts, in two languages, doesn't scale.

        Known gaps: 
        English `sentiment` and `buy_again` hedge (return "mixed"/null) far more often under
        the current models than the previous ones — accuracy on the answers the model DOES

        backhanded compliments scores lower. Short reviews (<10 words) occasionally miss
        fields.

Your customers' words, handled carefully

      

        Every claim below is verified against the running system or a provider's own primary
        legal document — not marketing copy. See the full trace in our public repo.
      


      

        

          

            DEFAULT
            Stateless by default
          

          

            On the default plan, submitted review text and extraction output are never written
            to our database or application logs — the response you get back is the only
            copy. Proven, not asserted: verified by sending a distinctive sentinel string
            through the live API and confirming it appears in zero tables and zero logs.
          

          Read the verification →
        


        

          
No training on your data

          

            Quoted directly from our LLM provider's own Services Agreement: "Groq is not
            permitted to use Inputs or Outputs for training or fine-tuning any AI Model
            Services or other models, unless explicitly granted permission or instructed by
            Customer." Independently verified against their primary legal document, not
            a summary page.
          

        


        

          
Zero data retention on inference

          

            Inference-APIs Zero Data Retention is enabled on our LLM provider account. In the
            interest of the same honesty standard we hold every claim on this page to: our
            provider exposes no API-level way to independently re-verify this setting (no
            header, no endpoint) — this is our account holder's own Console
            confirmation, not something we can prove from outside.
