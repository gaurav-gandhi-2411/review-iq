        Known gaps: 
        English `sentiment` and `buy_again` hedge (return "mixed"/null) far more often under the current models than the previous ones — accuracy on the answers the model DOES commit to is unchanged, but it commits less often, and flat accuracy charges that the same as a wrong answer. Hinglish shows the opposite pattern. On the 27 short reviews (under 10 words) in our held-out test set, when the model says a field is unclear, that call is right 93.3% of the time — the information usually genuinely isn't in the text. The real short-review issue is different: it commits to a value that does not match the reference labels on 48 of 324 field checks, most often on `language` (13), `topics` (11), `pros` (6). Only 10 of 324 were genuine silent misses. Separately: sarcastic or backhanded phrasing is rare in real marketplace reviews — 3 of 106 in our held-out set — too few to measure reliably, so we don't claim a number for it either way. One more, stated plainly: unlike the fields above, the fake-review flag has not been measured against this held-out set. Its only historical number (n=40, a smaller and older corpus) is not reproducible and predates this test set — treat it as an early-access signal, not a scored capability, until that changes.

Default Stateless by default

            

              On the default plan, submitted review text and extraction output are never written
              to our database or application logs — the response you get back is the only
              copy. Proven, not asserted: verified by sending a distinctive sentinel string
              through the live API and confirming it appears in zero tables and zero logs.

No training on your data

            

              Quoted directly from our LLM provider's own Services Agreement: "Groq is not
              permitted to use Inputs or Outputs for training or fine-tuning any AI Model
              Services or other models, unless explicitly granted permission or instructed by
              Customer." Independently verified against their primary legal document, not
              a summary page.
            

          


          

            
Zero data retention on inference

            

              Inference-APIs Zero Data Retention is enabled on our LLM provider account. In the

              confirmation, not something we can prove from outside.
